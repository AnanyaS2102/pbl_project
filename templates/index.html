Introduction

⚡

The Problem

Solar energy is clean but unpredictable. Weather variability makes energy output hard to forecast, creating challenges for grid management and energy planning.

🤖

Our Solution

A Hybrid AI system combining K-Means clustering with cluster-specific Random Forest models that adapts to current weather conditions for accurate predictions.

🎯

Key Innovation

Instead of one model for all conditions, we train separate specialized models for Sunny, Intermittent-Cloud, and Overcast weather — achieving 95-99% accuracy.

Methodology

1

Data Collection

Solar energy production data from France — hourly records with timestamps, production values in MWh, day/month/hour features.

2

Data Preprocessing

Cleaned raw data, handled missing values, removed noise, sorted by timestamp for accurate time-series analysis.

3

Feature Engineering

Created lag features (lag1, lag24), rolling mean & standard deviation over 6 hours to capture solar behavior patterns.

4

K-Means Behaviour Clustering

Applied K-Means (k=3) to classify solar conditions into Sunny, Intermittent-Cloud, and Overcast using production variability features.

5

Cluster-Specific Model Training

Trained separate Random Forest models for each cluster — each model specializes in predicting output for its specific weather condition.

6

Real-Time Forecasting

Auto-detects current solar cluster, selects appropriate model, generates prediction with adaptive error correction.
